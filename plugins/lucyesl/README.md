# LucyESL

Find English-teaching jobs in South Korea and see what they pay, from inside Claude. This
plugin connects LucyESL's MCP server and adds a skill that teaches Claude to read its
records the way LucyESL publishes them.

Ask for jobs by region, student age, housing, visa, schedule or minimum monthly pay; open
one listing's facts (pay in KRW, teaching and on-site hours, housing, visas, benefits,
start date, and what the advert left unstated); or quote the median advertised salary,
how many adverts state their hours and benefits, and where in Korea the jobs are.

Most listings are copied from other public job boards and are marked as copied; LucyESL
has not verified those employers. A listing posted on LucyESL by the employer comes from
an organisation LucyESL verified. Every tool is read-only, with no account and no key.
